Build With AI: Basics · Step 3 — PRD approved

Wayback

Snap your parking spot once. Get to your room, then back to your car, step by step, inside a building GPS can't see.

What changed since scope. Scope said: "AI matches the parking capture… shortest walking route there and back." Now we know exactly what that means. There's one Home screen. Every AI guess needs your ✓. Wrong guesses have a three-level safety net. Directions run one step at a time with the right lift glowing. Find my car, Help, and Arrived close the loop.

Look and Feel

  • Futuristic AR / game feel, like a sci-fi visor HUD, inspired by AR games like Pokémon GO
  • Dark background, glowing accent lines
  • The correct lift, door, or turn glows in each step
  • Playful, rounded "quest" touches
  • Style only: no live camera AR

The Safety Net

For every location match: parking, Find my car, and Help.

  1. AI guess → you ✓ or say "No"
  2. Pick from next best guesses (photos)
  3. Snap a sign board → match again
  4. Pick from a plain list assumption

No path dead-ends.

Features and Acceptance Criteria

Capturing the Parking Spot

Source: scope.md › The POC Boundary

  • Capture opens the camera, and one photo is sent for matching
  • The result shows a floor + zone label and the matched reference photo
  • Readable sign text (e.g. "B1") helps the guess
  • A rough GPS position is saved when available. Missing GPS never blocks or changes the result
  • ✓ saves it as Current Parking, and the Home card shows label, photo, and time
Fixing a Wrong Location
  • "No, that's wrong" → next best guesses as labelled photos, tap to confirm
  • "None of these" → asks for a photo of a sign board → matches again
  • Still failing → pick from a plain list assumption
  • Every path ends in a confirmed location
Guiding to a Room
  • After confirming, the app asks "Where are you going?" and accepts a typed room number
  • Shortest route on the dorm map, one step at a time
  • Each step: area picture with the correct choice highlighted (3 lifts, Lift 2 glowing) + one line ("Take Lift 2 up to Floor 5")
  • Next advances. The final step names the room
Finding the Car
  • Find my car → asks for a photo of where you are
  • Locate (with the same fixes) → shortest route to the saved spot
  • Works from any spot on the demo path, not just the room
  • The last step offers Arrived
Help While Walking
  • Help on every direction step assumption
  • Help → snap → locate → fresh steps to the same destination
Arriving and Parking History
  • Arrived moves Current Parking into history and empties the card
  • History rows show date, time, label, and photo, newest first, view-only
States
  • First use: empty card prompting Capture, and an empty history
  • No GPS underground: nothing changes for the user
  • Unknown room number: "don't know that room", ask again assumption
  • Reopen app: current parking and history are still there
  • Capture while already parked: open question
